Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

leve is a personal nutrition and weight-goal tracking app. This page explains what data the app reads from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS), and how that data is used.

What health data leve reads

  • Weight — used to fill in your weigh-in history automatically instead of you entering it by hand.
  • Active calories burned — used to fill in your daily activity total automatically instead of you entering it by hand.

leve does not write any data back to Health Connect or Apple Health.

Where this data goes

Nowhere but your own device. leve has no backend server for your health, nutrition, or goal data — everything is stored locally on your phone. Nothing you log, and nothing imported from Health Connect or Apple Health, is ever sent to leve's developer or to any third party.

Your control over this data

Importing is entirely opt-in. You can disconnect it at any time from Account > Settings, which stops future imports (it does not delete data already imported — you can remove individual entries from your diary the same way as any manually logged entry). You can also revoke leve's access entirely from your device's Health Connect or Health app settings.

One more feature, unrelated to health data

leve optionally offers an AI-powered food photo scanner. This is a separate, opt-in feature that is off by default and requires you to supply your own Anthropic API key. When used, a food photo is sent directly from your device to Anthropic's API to identify the food and estimate its nutrition — it does not pass through any server leve's developer operates, and it has no connection to your Health Connect or Apple Health data.

Questions

leve is developed and used personally by its developer, with a small number of invited testers. If you have questions about this policy, reach out to whoever invited you to test the app.
